💡 Our Vision

To build a highly secure, transparent, accessible and independently verifiable electronic voting system that individuals, organizations, institutions, communities and governments across the world can adapt, trust and use.

We envision a future where participating in an election does not have to be limited by geographical boundaries, physical infrastructure or traditional voting procedures.

A future where technology makes elections more accessible while preserving the fundamental principles of democracy: one eligible voter, one vote, privacy, transparency, accountability and trust.

🎯 Our Mission

We are building an open-source electronic voting ecosystem designed to transform how elections are conducted.

Our mission is to bring developers, cybersecurity professionals, researchers, designers, election administrators and technology enthusiasts together to develop, test, improve and maintain a reliable e-voting solution.

This is bigger than a software project. It is a collaborative effort to explore how technology can improve electoral processes worldwide.

🌐 Why E-Voting?

Traditional election processes can involve significant logistical, administrative and financial challenges.

Technology presents opportunities to address some of these challenges through carefully designed digital systems.

Our proposed system aims to explore solutions that support:

- Accessibility: Enable eligible voters to participate through secure and accessible digital channels.
- Security: Protect election infrastructure, voter identities and voting records against unauthorized access and manipulation.
- Transparency: Make election procedures and results independently verifiable.
- Privacy: Protect the secrecy of individual votes.
- Accuracy: Ensure votes are recorded, stored and counted according to documented election rules.
- Scalability: Support elections of different sizes, from small associations to large institutions.
- Adaptability: Allow organizations and jurisdictions to configure the system according to their election requirements.
- Accountability: Maintain auditable records of administrative and election-related activities without compromising ballot secrecy.

🔐 Security Is Our Priority

An electronic voting system must earn public trust.

We recognize that election security goes beyond login authentication, encryption or a well-designed interface.

Our development approach will prioritize:

- Strong identity and voter eligibility verification.
- Ballot secrecy and voter privacy.
- Protection against duplicate voting.
- Secure cryptographic protocols.
- Tamper-evident election records.
- Protection against insider threats and unauthorized administrative actions.
- Independent security assessments and penetration testing.
- Reproducible builds and transparent development practices.
- Verifiable election results.
- Documented incident response and recovery procedures.

Our commitment: Security will be an ongoing engineering responsibility, not a marketing statement.

No implementation will be considered suitable for real-world high-stakes elections simply because it works in a demonstration environment. Independent evaluation, operational safeguards and applicable legal requirements will be essential.

📌 How Contributions Will Work

We intend to use GitHub Issues and Pull Requests to organize development transparently.

The contribution process will follow these steps:

1. Identify an issue: Browse existing GitHub Issues or propose a new improvement, feature or bug report.
2. Apply to contribute: Express your interest in an issue and explain how you intend to approach it.
3. Review and assignment: Project maintainers will review applications, clarify requirements and assign suitable contributors.
4. Development: Work on the approved task in a dedicated branch.
5. Submit a Pull Request: Explain the implementation, reference the related issue and provide relevant tests.
6. Code review: Maintainers and other contributors review the submission.
7. Resolution: Approved changes are merged, and the associated issue is closed.

Please do not begin substantial work on an issue that requires assignment without first receiving approval.
